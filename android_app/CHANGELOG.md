# Changelog

All notable changes to the BioDINO-Edge Android application are documented in this file.

## v1.0.1 - 2026-11-11
### Documentation & Synchronization
* **Paper Title & Alignment:** Updated paper title and citations to *BioDINO-Edge: Gradient-Free Cascades of Frozen Vision Foundation Models for On-Device Few-Shot Plant Disease Diagnosis*.
* **Repository Synchronization:** Synchronized master branch and tag v1.0.1 with full assets and release documentation.

## v1.0.0 - 2026-11-06
### Highlights
* **Initial Stable Release:** Official launch of the BioDINO-Edge mobile application.
* **On-Device Edge Inference:** Real-time plant disease diagnosis powered by ONNX Runtime.
* **Dual-Tier Model Architecture:** Built-in lightweight baseline model (~22 MB) with optional in-app download for the Tier-2 companion model (~85 MB).
* **Multi-Language Support:** Full localization for English, French, German and Persian.
