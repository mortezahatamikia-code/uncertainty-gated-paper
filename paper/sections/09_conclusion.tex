\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}
We presented \textbf{BioDINO-Edge}, a gradient-free few-shot framework that adapts frozen DINOv2 and BioCLIP foundation models for on-device plant disease diagnosis without backpropagation. Closed-form Ledoit--Wolf shrinkage resolves small-sample covariance singularity ($N \ll d$), down-weighting shared vegetative background variance and enabling Fisher-LDA projection in $\approx$0.53\,s on a mobile CPU. An adaptive confidence gate combining normalized Shannon entropy and top-2 margin routes routine foliage to a 59.78\,ms Tier-1 path ($\approx$16.7\,FPS), escalating ambiguous queries to a multimodal Tier-2 classifier with closed-form transductive recalibration. The system ships as a modular Android client and executes both tiers on the phone CPU without network access. It reaches 97.41\% $\pm$ 0.71\% accuracy on PlantVillage with a 0.702\% false-negative rate, and 84.22\% $\pm$ 11.24\% in 5-way 5-shot testing on PlantDoc.
