\begin{abstract}
On-device plant disease diagnosis is held back by scarce annotations, background clutter, and edge backpropagation cost. We propose \textbf{BioDINO-Edge}, a gradient-free few-shot framework that adapts frozen vision foundation models without fine-tuning or graphics processing unit (GPU) acceleration. It combines DINOv2 lesion-contour patches with BioCLIP taxonomic embeddings aligned to cached botanical prompts in 896 dimensions. Because support is scarce relative to the high-dimensional embedding space, the empirical covariance becomes singular, and shared leaf background hides localized symptoms. BioDINO-Edge therefore applies analytical Ledoit--Wolf shrinkage to build regularized Fisher linear discriminant projections. This closed-form step suppresses shared vegetative variance and solves the discriminant in 0.53\,s on a mobile central processing unit (CPU) with no backpropagation; the surrounding support-feature extraction, not the algebra, dominates offline calibration cost.

At inference, an uncertainty gate uses normalized Shannon entropy and top-2 margin to send routine foliage through a 59.78\,ms Tier-1 fast path ($\approx$16.7 frames per second (FPS)) on an Android handset, while only ambiguous queries go to multimodal Tier-2. High-confidence predictions are buffered as anchors to recalibrate Tier-2 projections transductively. BioDINO-Edge is packaged as a modular on-device client that operates fully offline and reaches 97.41\% $\pm$ 0.71\% 30-episode accuracy (peak 98.67\%, macro F1 98.41\%) with a 0.702\% false-negative rate on 11-class PlantVillage. Against semi-supervised label propagation it trails by 1.08 percentage points on the controlled split when the whole test pool is visible, but that advantage requires the batch and it does not transfer: labelling one leaf at a time costs the propagator 3.00 points, and on the field benchmark BioDINO-Edge leads by 18.65 points in 27-way evaluation and 22.29 points at 5-way 5-shot. On in-the-wild PlantDoc, it reaches 84.22\% $\pm$ 11.24\% in 5-way 5-shot testing and 50.79\% in 27-way evaluation. Cascading frozen foundation models with closed-form adaptation therefore offers a practical, low-latency route to autonomous plant pathology at the edge without cloud dependence.
\end{abstract}

\begin{highlights}
\item Gradient-free adaptation of frozen DINOv2 and BioCLIP needs no backpropagation.
\item Uncertainty gate pairs a 60\,ms fast visual path with a 165\,ms deep tier.
\item Recalibration solves in 0.53\,s after feature caching, 11\,s from raw pixels.
\item 97.41\% accuracy on PlantVillage at 0.702\% false negatives.
\item Closed-form recalibration needs no query-set graph, unlike propagation.
\end{highlights}
