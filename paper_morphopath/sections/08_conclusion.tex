\section{Conclusion and Future Work}
\label{sec:conclusion}
\balance
We introduced \textbf{MorphoPath-Edge}, an autonomous, zero-parameter few-shot foliar diagnostic framework engineered for low-power edge hardware. By decoupling macroscopic foliar geometry into \textbf{Four Botanical Archetypes} (Solanoid, Vitoid, Pooid, Rosoid) atop frozen DINOv2 and BioCLIP models, MorphoPath-Edge elevates autonomous in-the-wild routing accuracy from 79.35\% to \textbf{93.25\%} (100\% Top-2 recall), resolving cascading taxonomic collapse and eliminating human-in-the-loop dependencies. Our closed-form \textbf{Test-Time Manifold Rectification} theorem algebraically cancels illumination centroid shifts ($\|\Delta\|_2 \approx 0.30$) in 0.041\,ms with zero learned parameters. Intra-archetype metric spaces are stabilized via Ledoit--Wolf covariance shrinkage ($\lambda=0.60$) and micro-symptom prompt ensembles, while Cardinality-Equalized Bayesian Triage reduces false-positive fungicide alerts by 38.1\%.

Across 30-episode Monte Carlo evaluations on in-the-wild PlantDoc, MorphoPath-Edge establishes a new state of the art with \textbf{62.30\% Top-1} and \textbf{80.95\% Top-2} accuracy, outperforming autonomous hierarchical baselines by \textbf{+11.84\%} ($p = 1.03 \times 10^{-21}$) and surpassing human oracle models. On PlantVillage, it delivers \textbf{99.20\%} Top-2 accuracy on emerging cultigens and \textbf{94.25\%} across all 38 classes simultaneously. Operating on mobile CPUs via INT8 quantization, the system achieves \textbf{18.66\,FPS (53.60\,ms latency)}, a peak RAM heap of \textbf{66.95\,MB}, and a standalone APK of \textbf{22.88\,MB} with zero added neural weights. Future work will extend the framework to multimodal hyperspectral aerial scouting and closed-loop robotic micro-spraying implements.
